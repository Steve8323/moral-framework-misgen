Headline (straight answer to "make sure it's a local minimum"): The models are genuine local minima (Hessian minimum eigenvalue > 0; with weight decay they are true finite minima). But the strong version of the thesis — "by sheer chance two converged models split into one aligned + one misaligned" — does NOT reproduce in this toy. At genuine minima the generalization outcome is pinned by the data statistics, not by the random seed (seed std ~0.02–0.06 everywhere; never bimodal). And two earlier results were partly artifacts (below). The thesis is real in actual LLMs (literature), but this toy is too simple to show it.

1. Are they local minima? — Yes, with one caveat

  • Hessian check (my run): via Hessian-vector-product power iteration, the minimum eigenvalue is positive (+7×10-6) for both low- and high-misgen models — no negative-curvature escape direction, so local minima, not saddles.
  • Caveat (both audits + me agree): with plain cross-entropy on separable data there is no finite minimum — weights grow without bound (||W|| 6→12→26 over 600/6k/30k steps) along a margin-maximization plateau. These are stable approximate critical points (grad norm 10-2→10-5), not strict minima.
  • Genuine finite minima: adding weight decay (1e-3) stabilizes ||W|| (~3.5) and drives grad norm low — true local minima. All checks below use these.

2. Does chance split aligned vs misaligned at genuine minima? — No (in this toy)

In this toy the behaviorally-relevant minimum is effectively unique given the data.

3. Corrections to earlier experiments (what the audits caught)

Experiment 3 ("kind of minimum predicts goal-misgen") — does NOT hold as stated.
  • Early-stopping confound: Adam's "low misgen" (0.44) was an artifact of stopping at 800 steps. Trained longer it climbs 0.20→0.49→0.76→0.92 (right panel above). At genuine (weight-decayed) minima the 2× spread collapses and Adam, SGD, SAM all land at high misgen (0.78–0.92).
  • Sharpness sign was scale-dependent: audit found the "sharper→more misgen" (r=+0.40) only appears at the non-standard large perturbation scale; under standard small-scale / CE sharpness it reverses (r=−0.85) and the real correlate is weight norm / training progress (grad-norm r=−0.92), not curvature.
Experiment 2 zero barrier — an arithmetic artifact, not mode connectivity. The agreement set is solvable with either cue block alone, so interpolating a consequentialist (C-reader) with a deontologist (D-reader) gives a model using both — which is better, dropping midpoint loss below the endpoints. The "barrier = 0" is forced by that redundancy; it is not evidence the two solutions share a basin. (The loss-equivalence + off-distribution divergence themselves are real and persist.)
What survives, robustly: Part 1 hedging (redundant cues → ~0.49, 0 committed, stable under 50× training and weight decay). Part 2 loss-equivalence + maximal off-distribution divergence (0.975 vs 0.022). The existence of a large OOD spurious-cue failure, and that SAM does not fix it. All reproduced bit-for-bit by both audits.

4. So is your thesis wrong? — No, the TOY is just too simple

The thesis (chance → one model generalizes, another misgeneralizes, at equal loss) is empirically real in large models: McCoy et al. 2020 trained 100 BERTs identical except seed — same in-domain accuracy (83.6–84.8%) but 0% to 66% on a syntactic OOD probe; D'Amour et al.'s underspecification shows the same across vision/NLP/medicine. That is genuine seed-driven multiplicity.

Our MLP fails to show it because it is under-expressive on a near-linearly-separable task: there is essentially one behaviorally-distinct solution given the data, so seeds converge to the same function. To demonstrate the thesis at genuine minima you need a regime with a real Rashomon set — multiple loss-equivalent and behaviorally-distinct solutions that the seed selects among. Candidates: (a) a higher-capacity model on a task with two genuinely competing mechanisms (the BERT/HANS structure), (b) two equally-clean competing cues (not one easy + one hard), so symmetry-breaking is real, or (c) directly the LM scale-up with matched moral dilemmas.
